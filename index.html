Questions 1–4

Relationships and ratios

1Additive or Multiplicative? Which table shows an ADDITIVE relationship?

Multiplicative means you multiply the top by the same number to get the bottom every time.

Worked solution

D. The top goes up by 2 and the bottom goes up by 2. Each bottom number is the top number plus 5, so y = x + 5. That is additive.

A multiplies by 4 (1×4 = 4, 3×4 = 12). B multiplies by 3. C multiplies by 5. Those three are multiplicative.

2A school orders 4 blue notebooks for every 7 red notebooks. Select TWO ratios equivalent to 4:7.

Multiply both numbers by the same number.

Worked solution

B and C. 4×3 = 12 and 7×3 = 21, so 12:21. 4×4 = 16 and 7×4 = 28, so 16:28.

A (8:12) and D (20:30) do not use the same multiplier for both numbers. E (7:4) flips the ratio.

3A fruit punch recipe uses 3 cups of orange juice for every 5 cups of pineapple juice. How many cups of pineapple juice are needed to make 40 cups of punch?

How many cups are in one batch of punch?

Worked solution

C. 25 cups. One batch is 3 + 5 = 8 cups. 40 ÷ 8 = 5 batches. Pineapple juice is 5 × 5 = 25 cups.

4A team has 2 seventh-graders for every 3 eighth-graders. Which table is correct?

The correct table multiplies both numbers by 2, then 3, then 4. The other table just adds.

Worked solution

A. (2, 3), (4, 6), (6, 9), (8, 12). Both numbers are multiplied by 2, then 3, then 4.

B, (2, 3), (4, 5), (6, 8), (8, 11), does not keep the 2 to 3 ratio.

Tables, equations, and totals

5Which situation matches?

x24610

Divide a bottom number by the top number above it. The situation should use that same “for every” relationship.

Worked solution

A. y = 3x. 2×3 = 6, 4×3 = 12, 6×3 = 18, and 10×3 = 30. Mia earns $3 for every $1 Jay earns.

6Group A: rectangle length is 6 units greater than width. Group B: length is 3 times width. Write an equation for each (width = x, length = y). Which is MULTIPLICATIVE? Explain.

Which group is MULTIPLICATIVE?

“Greater than” means add. “Times” means multiply.

Worked solution

Group A: y = x + 6. y = 6 + x means the same thing. The length is 6 more than the width, so this one adds.

Group B: y = 3x. y = 3*x and y = x*3 mean the same thing.

Group B is multiplicative because you multiply the width by 3 every time.

7A bakery uses 2 cups of flour for 16 muffins. How many cups for 80 muffins?

Flour (cups)0246?
Muffins016324880

How many groups of 16 muffins are in 80? Each group uses 2 cups of flour.

Worked solution

10 cups. 80 ÷ 16 = 5 groups of muffins. 5 × 2 = 10 cups of flour.

8Box A: 2 chocolate cookies for every 5 sugar cookies. Box B: 3 peanut butter cookies for every 4 sugar cookies. If EACH box has 20 sugar cookies, which box has more TOTAL cookies? Show your work and explain.

Which box has more total cookies?

Each box has 20 sugar cookies. Scale the other cookies, then add them to 20.

Worked solution

Box A: 20 ÷ 5 = 4 groups, so chocolate cookies = 2 × 4 = 8. Total = 8 + 20 = 28.

Box B: 20 ÷ 4 = 5 groups, so peanut butter cookies = 3 × 5 = 15. Total = 15 + 20 = 35.

Box B has more total cookies, because 35 is more than 28.

Questions 9–12

Rates, percent, and a bigger bag

9Movie tickets cost $6 each. Complete the table, graph the ordered pairs on your paper, and write an equation for y in terms of x.

Tickets (x) 12358
Cost (y)

Each ticket costs $6. Multiply the number of tickets by 6.

Worked solution

The costs are 6, 12, 18, 30, and 48. The equation is y = 6x. y = 6*x and y = x*6 mean the same thing.

On your paper, plot (1, 6), (2, 12), (3, 18), (5, 30), and (8, 48). The points line up on a straight line through (0, 0).

10A student reads 120 pages in 40 minutes at a constant rate. Complete the table. What is the unit rate in pages per minute?

Time (min) 510204060
Pages 120

Divide 120 pages by 40 minutes. That is the pages in one minute.

Worked solution

Pages: 15, 30, 60, 120, 180. The unit rate is 3 pages per minute, because 120 ÷ 40 = 3. Then 5×3 = 15, 10×3 = 30, 20×3 = 60, and 60×3 = 180.

11A grid has 100 equal squares. 65 are shaded. What percent is shaded?

The grid has 100 squares. The number shaded is the percent.

Worked solution

C. 65%. 65 shaded squares out of 100 is 65%.

12A bag has 5 red, 4 blue and 3 green marbles. A larger bag has 60 marbles in the SAME ratio. How many are green?

Add the red, blue, and green marbles first. Then see how many of those groups fit in 60.

Worked solution

B. 15 green marbles. 5 + 4 + 3 = 12 marbles in the small bag. 60 ÷ 12 = 5 groups. Green is 3 × 5 = 15.
