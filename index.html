SCIENCE - 6

Raylee Mclain

Read about states of matter. Then write short definitions in your own words. A few words for each word is enough.

Reading

Matter is anything that has mass and takes up space. In a solid, particles stay close together and vibrate in place, so a solid keeps its shape. In a liquid, particles slide past one another, so a liquid takes the shape of its container. In a gas, particles move quickly and spread out to fill the container. Adding heat makes particles move faster. When a solid gains enough heat, it melts into a liquid. When a liquid gains enough heat, it evaporates into a gas. Taking heat away slows particles down. A gas can condense into a liquid, and a liquid can freeze into a solid.
